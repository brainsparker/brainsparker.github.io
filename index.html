Work

Three messy product problems, what we changed, and what happened next.

Making search feel more like a conversation

You.com began as a traditional search engine with a layer of AI tools. Research made the opportunity clear: people wanted to ask follow-ups and shape their intent in conversation. I led the move to a chat-first experience with answers grounded in sources.

Monthly users grew from about 1 million to 10 million as chat became the main experience.

Fixing the messy plumbing behind search

Crate & Barrel’s search, inventory, and product data didn’t always agree. That made discovery frustrating for shoppers and expensive for the business. I led the rebuild underneath it all so the same trustworthy information powered every channel.

The new platform delivered seven figures in ROI within 30 days of launch.

Helping buyers feel confident about a choice

G2’s buyers had plenty of software options. The harder part was knowing which one was right. I owned the search roadmap across ranking, personalization, recommendations, and AI-powered guidance.

Core buyer engagement increased 145% year over year.
